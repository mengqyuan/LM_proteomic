# Integrated human CSF multi-omics reveals diagnostic biomarkers and distinct molecular subtypes for leptomeningeal metastasis

 Leptomeningeal metastasis (LM) is a devastating complication of systemic cancer that remains difficult to diagnose. We aimed to characterize the cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) multi-omics landscape to identify diagnostic biomarkers and molecular subtypes. LM remains difficult to diagnose, and its molecular heterogeneity is incompletely characterized. This multicenter study integrates CSF proteomics, targeted metabolomics, and public single-cell transcriptomic data to delineate four LM-associated functional axes. The resulting molecular landscape provides a biological context for diagnostic biomarker discovery and patient stratification. A ten-protein panel achieved high diagnostic accuracy in discovery cohort and two independent validation cohorts. Proteomic subtyping identified a highly proliferative, tumor-dominant subtype and a less proliferative, immune-rich subtype with distinct survival outcomes. These findings extend CSF profiling beyond individual biomarkers by capturing both shared disease-associated changes and interpatient heterogeneity. Together, these findings highlight the potential of CSF profiling for diagnosis and patient stratification in LM.
